We deliver
across Europe.

Every delivery route is planned individually — costs, timelines and permits differ by country. We handle the full logistics chain from our production facility in Siberia to your plot, including customs documentation and country-specific import permits.

Typical transit to Central Europe runs 18–28 days by rail and road. We send live tracking links the moment your shipment departs, and a project coordinator stays reachable throughout.
